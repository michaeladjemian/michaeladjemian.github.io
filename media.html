For journalists

Media

I regularly speak with reporters on the economics of commodity markets, food prices, and agricultural and trade policy. I can explain how supply shocks, government reports, tariffs, and transportation costs move the prices that farmers receive and that households pay at the grocery store. I'm glad to provide background, context, or on-the-record commentary, and I can often respond on deadline.

For interview requests, contact me at michael.adjemian@uga.edu.
