Professional services

Consulting

I am available for consulting engagements in my areas of expertise, including commodity markets, price analysis and forecasting, risk management, derivatives, and agricultural and trade policy. My work draws on more than a decade of experience in academia and the federal government, including service at USDA's Economic Research Service, the White House Council of Economic Advisers, and as a consultant to the Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

Areas of engagement

How I Can Help

Typical engagements include economic analysis and expert reports, price and market assessments, evaluation of policy or regulatory questions, and litigation support and related advisory work. I have provided analysis in matters involving commodity and derivatives markets, and can advise clients in the private sector, government, and legal settings.
